This paper proposes a unified conceptual framework to clarify three often-conflated concepts in genomic prediction for plant breeding: multimodality (integrating diverse data types), interaction modeling (capturing non-additive effects like epistasis), and multimodule architectures (modular computational designs). PloS one 2026--01 Medium

Highly contiguous reference genome assembly of the endangered Orcés' blue whiptail Holcosus orcesi.

This paper presents the first high-quality reference genome assembly for Holcosus orcesi, a critically endangered lizard endemic to a small region in southern Ecuador. Using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing, the authors produced a 1.68 Gb assembly with only 91 contigs, an N50 of 76.2 Mb, and 96.8% BUSCO completeness, making it one of the most contiguous squamate genomes to date. This resource will support conservation genomics and evolutionary studies of this rare montane lineage.
